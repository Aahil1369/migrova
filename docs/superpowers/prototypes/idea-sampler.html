THE COVER · OPENING AND CLOSING

LAST OPEN PAGE · "OBSERVATIONS"

Your own route, drawn in foil

Real passports have an "Observations" page. Yours carries a short note in your words and your three legs drawing one by one — Gilgit-Baltistan → Kampala → USA — ending at a dot: "here, building this". The founder story without an About page.

THE 5 TOOLS · ONE DECISION

Rubber stamps, or stick-on visa stickers?

My prototype uses rubber stamps for the tools. The judge flagged that OpportuMap's hero already has a rubber visa stamp — so Migrova could use stick-on visa vignettes instead (the full-page stickers real visas are printed on, with a hologram stripe), applied with a lift-and-settle motion. Keeps the two apps distinct: "OpportuMap stamps; Migrova inspects."

Bigger alternatives (if you'd rather not do the passport)

Under the LampNo page-turning: the homepage is one security document — guilloche that prints itself, microprint you read with a loupe, and a UV lamp that reveals the hidden truth on every topic (the real fee, the lawyer red flag, the fake site).
Red-Eye to DaylightThe scroll is one overnight flight. A little plane flies past the 5 tools as waypoints, a window-shade opens on dawn, your 3-leg route is the climax, and it lands on a prefilled From → To search.
Unfold the WorldHonours the 1922 Nansen passports for stateless refugees: a long folded sheet unfolds panel by panel as you scroll, flips into a night map where 418 official sources light up like sunrise, then refolds into the button.

Things I'll protect no matter what

Readable & normal to scroll — the passport is the background (as you asked); all text stays real, scrollable, searchable content on top — no trapped scrolling.
Fast on cheap phones — a lighter mode on low-end devices; phones see one passport page at a time.
Never looks like a fake government document — called a "Journey Book" issued by Migrova, no national crest, tiny "SPECIMEN · NOT A TRAVEL DOCUMENT" print. (Important: Migrova's whole promise is "no fakes".)
Heavy effects on the homepage only — visa/relocation reports get light echoes (a hologram ✓ badge on official links, a page-number tab), not scroll effects.
Copy fix — "100 countries" → 195 everywhere.
